Local imports are for your browser only; published ForeverDB records are separate. Accepted format: an array or an object with an items array (up to 50,000 records).

ForeverDB client snapshots may contain speculative or event-only items; a catalog record is not confirmation that it can be obtained in-game. Unmapped stats, item effects, procs, set bonuses and random suffixes may require manual checks. Estimated weights do not turn these into verified damage formulas.

Verification sources, mechanics & important limitations

Sources last reviewed 3 November 2026. Verified here means the cited Forever documentation establishes the relationship, not that a complete combat simulation has been validated.

  • Blizzard: Forever Deep Dive Panel Recap — September 2026: unified hit/crit, new expertise and avoidance reduction, bonus healing provides one-third as much bonus damage.
  • Blizzard: Hunter & Druid Deep Dive — September 30, 2026: spec trees, pet stat scaling, Careful Aim (20% Int to AP per rank), Lone Wolf (20% player attack damage without pet), Feral/Balance/Restoration updates, including Cat/Bear inheriting equipped weapon DPS in form while using fixed form attack speeds.
  • Blizzard: Mage & Shaman Deep Dive — October 7, 2026: Arcane/Fire/Frost design and talents.
  • Blizzard: Forever beta notes — through October 26, 2026: latest talent and racial changes. Includes Oct 1 beta level 30, superseded by 1–60 launch.
  • Blizzard: Return to Azeroth in Forever — October 29, 2026: launch level 1–60.
  • Blizzard: Create the Hero You Want to Be — September 22, 2026: confirms all nine playable classes.
  • Armory WoW Forever talent calculator — updated October 31, 2026: nine class trees (fan-extracted build 1.60.1.70707). All added-class stat weights in this tool are user-editable hypotheses.
  • Bind on Forever item database — accessed November 2, 2026: game-client build 1.60.1.70707, source for embedded high-level item snapshot.
  • ForeverWoW Level 29 Frost Mage and Level 30 Hunter — data retrieved November 2, 2026, build 1.60.1.70532, source for lower-level sample items.
  • Wowhead: Forever Marksmanship Hunter guide — accessed November 2, 2026: test-documented pet inheritance: +2 pet HP per Stamina, 30% of armor, 10% of highest AP, 100% of crit. These are third-party reported figures, not official Blizzard numerical tables.

Not verified as a complete damage model: per-level primary-stat-to-AP/crit/health/mana tables; spell coefficients by rank, hit caps and target level; glancing blows, armor formula, defense/expertise conversions, weapon-speed normalization, talent interactions, haste on gear, enemy resistance and PvP mitigation, pet DPS shares, per-school bonuses, and a full encounter simulator. Feral weapon-DPS inheritance is documented, but a full Feral damage model is not. Weapon speed is tracked but given 0 weight by default. Crit/hit/haste rating is entered as a raw number with a default score weight of zero because Forever-specific conversions are not verified; tooltip percentages are separate fields. Equip/use effects, set bonuses and enchants are not automatically modeled. No talent is presumed taken except those explicitly selected; pet stat modeling for Warlock and other untoggled talents is not modeled; interactions not listed above are estimates.
